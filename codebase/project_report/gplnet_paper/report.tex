\begin{abstract}
This report documents the design, mathematical formulation, implementation, and experimental evaluation of GeoPhysicsLandslideNet (PS-GPLNet), a pentastream physics-closed network for binary landslide segmentation from optical and topographic remote sensing inputs. The architecture embeds Taylor-stabilized infinite-slope factor-of-safety logic throughout encoding, fusion, decoding, and dual-path output equilibrium. Experiments on Bijie and Landslide4Sense demonstrate strong performance against a broad baseline ladder. Implementation lives in \texttt{codebase/Geo\_physics\_equation\_derived\_model/from\_first\_steps/}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Landslide mapping from remote sensing is a central problem in hazard assessment. Purely convolutional segmenters often confuse spectrally similar terrain; dual-stream networks improve matters by separating RGB and DEM pathways, yet their fusion gates remain statistical. Physics-informed geo-AI has advanced susceptibility mapping, but dense segmentation with a closed geotechnical inductive bias spanning the full U-Net-like pipeline is still uncommon.

This report presents PS-GPLNet as an independent architecture: five encoders, Complementary Modality Bridges, MAO-GeoEGCA and TTEB fusion, dual physics decoders with PGDI, and Mechanistic Path Equilibrium Fusion (MPEF). DiGATe-style dual-stream gated U-Nets are used only as baselines.

\section{Related Work}
We position PS-GPLNet against (i) classical and transformer segmentation backbones (U-Net, DeepLabV3+, TransUNet, SegFormer), (ii) dual-stream gated multimodal landslide networks, and (iii) physics-informed susceptibility models that inject Newmark/FS constraints into losses rather than into dense decode routing. The distinguishing claim is architectural closure of FS cells from inlet to outlet.

\section{Methodology}
\subsection{Factor-of-safety cell}
\[
\mathrm{FS}=\frac{c+\phi h\cos^2\alpha}{\gamma h\sin\alpha\cos\alpha+w_m m+\varepsilon},\quad
\mathrm{FE}=\mathrm{ReLU}(1-\mathrm{FS}).
\]
Positive parameters use bounded exponentials for stability during training.

\subsection{System pipeline}
RGB and DEM EfficientNets provide appearance/topography features; physics encoders apply FS-gated cells; Prithvi-EO$+$LoRA supplies foundation context. CMB hybridizes CNN and physics per modality. MAO-GeoEGCA and TTEB fuse streams. Dual PhysicsDecoders upsample with PGDI; MPEF routes path logits by relative failure energy. Training uses deeply supervised Tversky loss plus MPEF entropy regularization.

A full algorithmic and mathematical description is provided in \texttt{from\_first\_steps/model\_architecture.md}.
